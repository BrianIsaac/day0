# Changelog

Version maintenance record for Day0, from the git history, grouped by release. Releases are tagged with semantic versions from 27 September 2026: `v0.1.0` is the build of 19 September 2026, and the work before it is grouped by date under "Before v0.1.0"; each engineering wave that reaches `main` takes the next minor, a hotfix on a release the next patch, and `v1.0.0` is the pilot release. Hashes are the commits on `main`, 1,607 of them at `f739614`; a merge of a job branch is listed by the substantive commits it carried, and counts are by author date. Every commit follows conventional-commit style, so `git log --no-merges --format='%ad %h %s' --date=short` is the full record and this file is its digest.

## v0.18.0, 9 October 2026

## v0.16.0, 5 October 2026

Wave 12, the work loop and supervision: the manager can stop a run that is working and pause one employee; a retry knows exactly what already landed, entry by entry, and sends what did not; nothing answers an anonymous caller with data; decisions can be made with Approve and Reject buttons in Slack as well as by the typed code, with nothing inbound opened; and the schema step retires the legacy fields and adds what the later waves read. Eight units (the schema step, the work loop, the guard, the manager channel, the pause, the joins, a fix unit and a fake Linear in the tree) landed on `staging` with the gate on the combined tree, reviewed as one change on three beds (the push from a `v0.15.0` volume in real mode, the hosted shape over production's own baseline, and the wave's walk against fake Slack with Socket Mode and the fake Linear), walked on real Slack, then the fixes that review and that walk asked for before the tag, each proven on a bed of its own; those fixes were walked again on real Slack, and what that walk found in the release's words was fixed and proven on a bed against fake Slack. 277 commits; 11,520 tests.

- **Stop (12-W).** A working card offers Stop, with an optional reason kept on the item; the run ends at its next fence and the item waits with Retry. A stop that meets an apply keeps every row it reported, and the apply reads its claim before each send, so after a Stop nothing more goes out and the rows it never reached are recorded as not sent; only a write on the wire is left to check. Stop also takes back an approval whose writes have not started, for as long as a pause holds them. A stopped item with nothing to check is closed without a retry.
- **The reconciliation, answered per entry (12-W).** Each write whose outcome is unknown is named by what it does and answered "It landed" or "It was not sent"; a write the ledger records as landed is shown as landed and asked nothing, and once you confirm it reads "Day0 recorded it as landed.", never as your answer: only the answers you give are sent and recorded; a retry never sends again a write that landed or was answered landed, and sends a write answered not sent, or recorded not sent, even on a thread or ticket another landed write carries (where nothing was answered not sent, a retry still never posts a second audit comment). A row reconciled before this release is asked again, entry by entry.
- **The pause (12-P).** Pause and Resume one employee on Manage (real mode): nothing new starts, every decision already asked stays answerable, an approval stands and its step runs at the resume; the home, the roster, the office and the Work tab say what the pause holds. The deployment's own pause (`./setup.sh pause`) holds queued work steps too, and an acknowledgement in Slack says the step runs at the resume rather than "now".
- **Nothing for an anonymous caller (12-G).** Every public function's first act is an ownership guard; a signed-out caller is answered only the release; `pnpm check:anonymous` asks every public function signed out and names any that answers.
- **Slack buttons (12-M).** Decision requests in Slack can carry Approve and Reject buttons. Each employee's own Slack app needs an app-level token, which a person generates in the app's settings and pastes on the employee's Slack card; the presses reach Day0 over Slack's Socket Mode through a new component, `slack-socket`, which setup starts in real mode; nothing inbound is opened.
